How to use the waveform
  • Click the waveform to move the playhead. Drag on empty space to create a segment.
  • Drag a segment's edges to resize it, or its body to move it. Delete removes the selected one, Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Mouse wheel zooms at the pointer, Shift + wheel (or the slider) scrolls sideways.
  • Space plays/pauses. Use Original / Lead removed to compare at the same position.
  • Anything outside the orange segments is left exactly as in your file.
  • The preview you hear is 16-bit so it starts instantly (it restarts after each edit). Export keeps full quality.
